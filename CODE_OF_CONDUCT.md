# Code of conduct

Be respectful. Assume good faith, disagree about the work and not the person, and keep feedback specific and useful.

Harassment, insults, personal attacks and discrimination are not welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, and lock threads or block users, when they break these rules.

To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or contact the maintainer through the profile at https://github.com/diegoaleyvag.
